HOW TO PLAY

  • Goal — eat every dot and power pellet to clear the level.
  • Power pellets — the big flashing dots turn ghosts blue for a few seconds. Eat them!
  • Ghost combo — each ghost eaten in one power window scores 200 → 400 → 800 → 1600.
  • Fruit — a bonus fruit appears twice per level for big points.
  • Lives — you start with 3 and earn +1 every 10,000 points.
  • 1000 levels — 100 worlds in 10 epochs. Ghosts get faster, house releases tighten, power-pellet windows shrink (6.8 s → 1.6 s) and the AI spends almost all its time chasing by the end.
